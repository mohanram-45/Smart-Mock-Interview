# Source-finding brief - Stage 1D batch 1 (Generative AI & RAG)
Goal: raise the share of records backed by GENUINE authoritative sources, without padding. You are given records (question, answers, `evidence_so_far`: claims already verified by executed code/derivation, or `background_only`).
For each record:
1. List the technical claims, design recommendations and factual comparisons in the answers that are NOT already covered by executed code/derivation (the `background_only` ones first, plus any API/library/default-value, algorithm-property, paper-result or documented-behaviour claim).
2. Find an authoritative source for each: official documentation (FAISS, Lucene/Elasticsearch/OpenSearch, Qdrant, Weaviate, pgvector, sentence-transformers, scikit-learn, Hugging Face, Ragas, vendor docs for general mechanisms) or the original research paper (arXiv / ACL anthology / SIGIR / etc.). Open it with WebFetch (or WebSearch then WebFetch). Quote the exact supporting sentence(s).
3. A source counts only if the quoted text genuinely supports the specific claim as worded. If it only supports a weaker or different claim, say `partially` and give a replacement that narrows our wording to what the source supports. If you cannot find support, say `not_found`; do not stretch. Do not cite blogs/marketing pages unless nothing authoritative exists, and then mark `secondary`.
4. If our text states something the source contradicts or that is stronger than the evidence, give an exact-substring fix (claim must occur exactly once in that field).
5. Do not cite anything you did not open. If WebFetch is refused for a page, say `unreachable` and do not cite it.
6. Many records are mechanism/arithmetic questions with nothing external to cite; it is fine for them to end with no source. Never add a citation just to have one.
Output a JSON list to the given path, one object per record: {"plan_id","claims":[{"claim","field","status":"supported|partially|not_found|unreachable|covered_by_execution","source_index":null|int,"note"}],"sources":[{"title","url","type":"official_doc|paper|secondary","quote","supports":"which claim(s) in our text it supports, in one sentence","status":"supported|partially"}],"fixes":[{"field","claim","problem","replacement"}],"residual_background_only":[...claims still unsupported]}.
Modify no other file. Finish with counts: records with >=1 supporting source, sources opened, unreachable, fixes.
