# Dataset Audit — SMART-MOCK-INTERVIEW

**Scope:** audit-only. No existing dataset, pipeline, model or app file was modified. All scripts live in `audit/` and write only to `audit/output/`.
**Audited files:** `data/raw/questions_seed.csv`, `data/raw/expanded_interview_questions.csv`, `data/interim/transformed.json`, `data/processed/questions_cleaned.json`, `data/processed/questions.csv`; pipeline code `src/components/{data_ingestion,data_transformation,data_cleaner,pipeline,config}.py`, `scripts/{expand_interview_dataset,clean_dataset_boilerplate}.py`; consumers `SiameseBiLSTM.py`, `model_registry.py`, `interview_session.py`, `Temp/evaluate_models.py`.

Evidence labels used throughout:
**[AUTO]** verified by a script that was run (all re-runnable via `python audit/run_audit.py`) · **[MANUAL]** I read the item(s) · **[SUSPECT]** plausible issue, not confirmed.

## 0. Verdict and executive summary

# ❌ FAIL — major quality and coverage issues must be resolved before RAG

| | |
|---|---|
| **Reported size** | 4,085 questions (counts reproduce exactly **[AUTO]**) |
| **Genuinely substantive Q&A** | **≤ 467** (11.4%) — the seed set. The other **3,618 (88.6%)** are deterministic template output with no technical content |

The 4,085 figure is real but misleading: it measures how many rows a script emitted, not how many interview questions exist. The pipeline is deterministic and well-structured, and the 467 seed items are a usable starting core — so this is fixable, but not by indexing what exists today.

### 1.4 The 30 "duplicates" the cleaner removed **[AUTO]**
All 30 are **cross-domain collisions** among generated rows (Data Analysis↔SQL: 20; ML↔Deep Learning: 10). 10 of the 12 templates don't mention the domain, so the same topic appearing in two domain lists yields the identical sentence (e.g. *"Compare hyperparameter tuning with a simpler alternative."*). The answers differed in all 30 cases. First occurrence wins by file order, so Data Analysis lost 10 of the 12 questions for each of `funnel analysis` and `data quality checks`, and Deep Learning lost 10 of 12 for `hyperparameter tuning` (the two templates that name the domain survive).

### 1.5 Template and boilerplate content **[AUTO]**
- **All 3,618** generated rows match one of 12 fixed question sentences; 0 fall outside them. 304 distinct (domain, topic) pairs, 12 questions each.
- Answers: only **15 distinct texts** once topic name, domain name and the domain-context phrase are masked. Topic-name tokens are **3.4%** of answer words on average.
- All 3,618 generated answers describe *what a good answer should contain* rather than giving one — 2,715 contain "A strong answer should…"/"a good candidate should…", the remaining Hard ones use "A strong advanced answer on X should…".
- Mean pairwise TF-IDF cosine between answers: same template, different topic **0.82**; random generated pair **0.30**; random seed pair **0.04**. 3,616 of 3,618 generated answers have another answer with cosine ≥ 0.80 (seed: 4 of 467).

Example **[MANUAL]**, SQL id 2667 (labelled **Hard**):
> Q: *What is window functions in SQL?*
> A: *Window Functions is a core SQL concept related to correct joins, aggregations, analytical logic, data modelling, and query efficiency. A strong answer should define the idea, explain why it matters, and mention a simple example…*

The answer never mentions `OVER`, `PARTITION BY` or any property of window functions. Because the sentences are topic-agnostic, they also produce nonsense such as *"Design an end-to-end solution that uses ORDER BY under production constraints."*, *"What is lists and tuples in Python?"*, *"Compare views with a simpler alternative."* (which alternative?) and *"What interview follow-up would you expect after explaining dashboard design?"* (301 rows; not a technical question).

### 3.3 Do Easy/Medium/Hard differ in complexity?
- **Generated (89%): no.** Mean answer words for template-matched rows (final labels): Easy 69.0 / Medium 66.9 / Hard 65.1. Hard answers are not longer, deeper or more specific; they differ only in which boilerplate sentence is used, identically for every topic (*"Design an end-to-end solution that uses ReLU under production constraints"*).

- **Role exposure [AUTO]:** the Hard pool an interview draws from is 93–100% template rows — Python Developer 100% (102 Hard, 0 seed); Data Analyst 98% (6 seed); Data Scientist 93% (59 seed). Easy pools are 60–87% template.

## 5. Semantic duplication analysis

Method: TF-IDF (1–2-gram) cosine on questions; clusters inspected by reading, not auto-merged. **Limitation:** lexical, not embedding-based; short or reworded duplicates fall below threshold. An embedding re-check is a recommended follow-up, not something this audit did.

| Threshold (seed only, 467) | Clusters | Rows |
|---|---:|---:|
| ≥ 0.95 | 4 | 8 |
| ≥ 0.85 | 7 | 14 |
| ≥ 0.75 | 14 | 28 |
| ≥ 0.65 | 21 | 49 |

**Inspection [MANUAL]:** at ≥ 0.75, 11 of 14 clusters are true duplicates; the other 3 are *not* (e.g. 4052/4053 *clustered vs non-clustered index*; 3734/3735 *what/why GANs*; 3659/3809). In the 0.65–0.75 band, 4 further true duplicate pairs (3686/3723, 3974/3990, 3675/3708, 3650/3694). **Confirmed: 15 duplicate pairs = 30 seed rows.** Three of them are exact duplicates differing only in punctuation/apostrophes that the cleaner's dedupe misses: 3770/3891 (*ROC… works?* / *works.*), 3816/3863 (*What's* / *What’s*), 3972/3983. Topic-level repeats I found by reading but TF-IDF did not cluster (overfitting ×3, autoencoders ×4, Boltzmann ×3, "deep learning vs ML" ×4+) are **[SUSPECT]** duplicates.

**Seed vs generated:** only 2 seed questions have a generated neighbour ≥ 0.8 and 25 ≥ 0.7. Typical pair: seed *"What is a LEFT JOIN?"* vs generated *"What is LEFT JOIN in SQL?"* (cos 0.77) — the **same question exists twice with two different answers**, one real and one boilerplate. Retrieval will not distinguish them.

**Answer duplication:** 3870 and 3955 are byte-identical; generated answers collapse to 15 texts (§1.5).

**Verdict:** "4,085 distinct questions" holds only for exact-string identity. Counting distinct *meaningful* items: ≤ 467 seed (minus ≥ 15 duplicate pairs) plus 304 topic names that carry no answer content.

## 7. RAG readiness

| Requirement | Finding |
|---|---|
| Trustworthy reference answer per question | **No for 88.6%** (content-free). For the 467 seed items: unverified, with ≥ 17 content-damaged answers confirmed and 44/121 reviewed defective |
| Enough information to judge a candidate | Generated: none. Seed: 126 answers < 20 words, all SQL ≤ 14 words, 36 open-ended cases < 30 words |
| Expected concepts extractable | Seed: partially (noun-phrase level, needs curation). Generated: **no** — only the topic name is topic-specific |
| Metadata adequate | **No.** Only `id, domain, difficulty, question, reference_answer`; id unstable, difficulty overwritten, no topic/source/type |
| Q&A record as retrieval unit | Yes in principle. Longest answer 276 words; by a rough words × 1.35 estimate 4 seed answers could exceed a 256-token embedding limit (not a tokenizer count) → **chunking not needed**, but check the chosen model's `max_seq_length` |
| Additional reference material required | **Yes.** Template rows add no facts; a grounded evaluator needs authoritative source passages (library docs, textbooks) as separately retrievable, cited chunks |
| Multiple valid answers | **Not handled.** No field for it; 43 open-ended seed questions carry one short answer (median 17 words) |
| Answer leakage into benchmarks | **Risk, see below** |

**Leakage / contamination [AUTO + SUSPECT]**
- `Temp/evaluate_models.py` uses 10 curated cases; **8 of the 10 questions appear verbatim in the dataset** (ids 3648, 3678, 3764, 3779, 3784, 3794, 4032, 4058). The harness uses hand-written references (TF-IDF cosine to the dataset's own reference: 0.10–0.74), so today's baseline numbers are not inflated by the dataset's answers — but any RAG benchmark built on the same questions while the index also contains them would be in-distribution.
- `SiameseBiLSTM` trains on **every** `reference_answer` and builds "negatives" from other reference answers labelled 0.15 (same domain) or 0.0. With ~89% of answers being near-identical templates (cosine ≈ 0.8 within domain), this supervision is noisy and contradictory. **[SUSPECT]** effect on model quality; I did not retrain or evaluate. Per your constraint, baseline models are untouched.
- **[SUSPECT]** Seed answers appear to come from public interview-prep lists (editorial text in 3697/3981, numbered "Solution:" blocks). Provenance and licensing are unrecorded. Needs your confirmation.
- No train/eval split field exists.

### Minimum metadata before embedding + FAISS
1. `qid` — stable, never reassigned (keep raw ids).
2. `source` — `seed | generated | authored`, plus `origin_id`.
3. `topic` — canonical topic string (needed for retrieval filters and the coverage report).
4. `difficulty` + `difficulty_basis` (`source | rubric | review`) — stop overwriting.
5. `question_type` — definition / comparison / scenario / coding / debugging / design / business.
6. `key_concepts` — list of expected concepts (the thing a RAG evaluator actually scores against).
7. `answer_status` — `verified | unverified | needs_fix | exclude`, with `quality_flags`.
8. `open_ended` (+ `accepted_alternatives`) for multi-answer questions.
9. `split` — `index | heldout_eval`, so benchmark questions are never retrievable.
10. Cleaned `reference_answer` (strip `Ans.`/`Solution:`/bleed/placeholder text).

Rows with `source=generated` should be flagged `exclude_from_index` until replaced.

## 10. Overall readiness verdict

**FAIL.** The dataset cannot serve as a RAG knowledge base in its current form:
- 88.6% of records contain no technical content, collapse to 15 answer texts, and would dominate retrieval for every topic.
- Difficulty labels are not trustworthy (44.2% overwritten; Hard in generated rows is determined by template wording).
- The only substantive content (≤ 467 items) has confirmed extraction damage, duplicates, wrong/misleading answers, and no verified-accuracy status; modern NLP/LLM/PyTorch/SQL-exercise/Power BI coverage is essentially absent.
- Metadata cannot support filtering, evaluation splits or traceability.

It would move to **PASS WITH FIXES** after steps 1–5 and 7 (a curated, adjudicated core with stable metadata) and to **PASS** only after the missing concepts (§8) exist as reviewed content with reference passages.

## Appendix B — Limitations
- No sentence embeddings were computed (package not installed; no dependencies added), so semantic-duplicate recall is lower than a SentenceTransformer analysis would give.
- Keyword-based coverage and question-type counts are heuristics over question text.
- 346 seed answers were not read; no claim is made about their correctness.
- I did not run the app, the frontend, the EDA notebook or any model, and did not read the `.docx` results report.
- Source/licensing of the seed content could not be determined from the files.
