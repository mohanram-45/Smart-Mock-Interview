# Behavioural interview content: source and licence policy

* **Origin (original content):** every competency description, rubric indicator, question and follow-up template in this folder was **written from scratch for this project**. Nothing was copied, paraphrased
  or scraped from a commercial question bank, a coaching product (including Yoodli) or any copyrighted interview guide. The STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) is a generic, long-established
  description technique and is used only as a way to look for evidence in an answer.
* **Licence:** the content is released under the same licence as the project's own code. Do not add third-party material here without recording its source and licence in this file.
* **Validation status:** **not human-validated.** Nobody has reviewed these rubrics against real interviewers or candidates. They are versioned (`version` in each JSON file) so a reviewer can propose changes; any change must bump the version.
* **What the rubrics may not assess:** protected characteristics (age, sex, gender, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, nationality, pregnancy, family status, sexual orientation), appearance, accent or
  dialect, inferred emotions or personality, health. The feedback builder refuses to mention them and a test checks the content for them.
* **What the engine does not do:** it gives no hire/reject decision, no numeric score and no percentage for any behavioural quality. It reports evidence found in the candidate's own words, what was not found, and a practice suggestion.
