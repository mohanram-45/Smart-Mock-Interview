Similarity score Sentence-BERT only

Compares your answer with a reference answer and gives a percentage. It measures closeness of wording and meaning, not correctness, and has not been checked against human markers. It appears only in the optional technical details of your feedback. New interviews use Sentence-BERT only: if it cannot load, the similarity is reported unavailable and no other scorer steps in.
